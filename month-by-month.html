Month by month

The queries with the largest share of Marketplace searches each month, next to how many searches found no apps at all. Press play or drag the month slider. In November 2024 broad queries such as “automation”, “jira” and “structure” lost most of their share in a single month, while app names held steady. Atlassian rolled out its redesigned Marketplace, with autocomplete and new categories, from mid-October to early November 2024, so shares before and after are not directly comparable. Queries are shown as Atlassian reports them; you can hide meaningless ones such as “1234” (see the method).
